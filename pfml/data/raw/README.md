# PFML policy dates

`pfml_policy_dates.csv` lists state paid family and medical leave programs with
enactment, contribution-start and benefit-start dates. **Compiled from memory
without web access**; before use in the paper verify every date against the
U.S. Department of Labor state PFML summary, the Bipartisan Policy Center
tracker, and each state statute. `cohort_asec_refyear` is the first calendar
year with benefits available for at least half the year, which is the
treatment cohort in ASEC reference-year time; `first_full_ref_year` is the
first complete benefit year (used for a robustness cohort definition).
States not listed are never treated through 2025.
